Construction is hard to teach for a simple reason: the most important things happen in an order, and most of them get covered up. A drawing shows the finished state. A photograph shows one moment. Neither shows how the building got there.

When I built the first SiteSeer models, the aim was learning material that showed the sequence as clearly as possible: one building, every stage, every layer, with nothing hidden that a learner needed to see.
