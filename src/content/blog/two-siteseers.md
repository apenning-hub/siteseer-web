SiteSeer started in construction education. For a decade it has helped people see how a building comes together, stage by stage, without needing to be on a live site. The thing that makes that work in a classroom turns out to matter just as much on a project: everyone looking at the same element, at the same stage, and understanding it before it's built.

So SiteSeer now comes in two versions.

## SiteSeer for education

For universities, TAFEs and schools. A real building at every stage of construction, from footings to fit-out, with inspection points that explain what you're looking at and why it's built that way. It runs in computer labs, inside learning platforms, on laptops and, now, on phones and tablets through the browser.

## SiteSeer for industry

For builders and project teams, as staff training. RFIs and defects cost the industry time and money, and many start the same way: someone on site not understanding how a detail goes together, because drawings don't show the sequence.

SiteSeer trains a team on the actual building before they build it. The model matches what gets built, one to one, so what people learn is what they'll find on site. Staged models come from a project's own architectural or shop-drawing models, or as a custom build by our team.

> Understand the detail before it becomes an RFI.

## The same foundation

Both versions share the same models, the same layer-by-layer approach and the same inspection points. A detail that teaches a first-year how a cavity tray drains is the same detail that shows a new crew how not to get it wrong.
